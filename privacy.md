## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how PDF Utility ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Camera and Photo Library Access
- **Purpose:** The app requests access to your device camera and photo library to allow you to scan documents and select images for PDF conversion.
- - **Storage:** Images and documents you select are processed locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or transmit these files to external servers.
  - - **Control:** You can revoke camera and photo library permissions at any time through your device settings.
   
    - ### 2. Local File Storage
    - - **Purpose:** PDF files created by the app are stored locally on your device for your access and management.
      - - **No Cloud Sync:** We do not sync, backup, or store your files on any cloud service or remote server.
        - - **Your Control:** You have complete control over your files and can delete them at any time.
         
          - ### 3. Subscription Information (RevenueCat)
          - - **Purpose:** We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions (monthly and yearly plans).
            - - **Data Collected:** RevenueCat may collect purchase history, subscription status, and device identifiers to manage your subscription.
              - - **Third-Party Policy:** RevenueCat's data practices are governed by their privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
               
                - ### 4. Advertising Data (Google AdMob)
                - - **Purpose:** We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the app.
                  - - **Data Collected:** AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve personalized ads.
                    - - **Ad Personalization:** You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings or Google's ad settings.
                      - - **Third-Party Policy:** Google AdMob's data practices are governed by their privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
                       
                        - ## How We Use Your Information
                       
                        - - **PDF Processing:** All document scanning and PDF creation happens entirely on your device. No files are transmitted to external servers.
                          - - **Subscription Management:** Subscription data is used to verify your access to premium features.
                            - - **Advertising:** Ad-related data is used to display relevant advertisements and support the free version of the app.
                             
                              - ## Data Sharing
                             
                              - We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:
                              - - **Service Providers:** RevenueCat and Google AdMob process data as described in their respective privacy policies.
                                - - **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.
                                 
                                  - ## Data Security
                                 
                                  - - All PDF processing occurs locally on your device
                                    - - We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data
                                      - - No user files are stored on our servers
                                       
                                        - ## Children's Privacy
